Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for mountains, snow, rivers and beautiful scenery.

Famous Places

Some popular places in Manali are Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass and Hadimba Temple.

Why I Like Manali

I like Manali because of its peaceful environment, natural beauty and pleasant weather.
